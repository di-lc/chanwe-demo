## Context

The company operates several service stations with multiple business units, high transaction frequency and a need for daily decisions around sales, suppliers, cash, inventory and commercial incentives.

## Problem

Operational information was fragmented and there was no integrated view of key metrics for short-term decision-making. This made it difficult to anticipate cash needs, negotiate with suppliers, assess the profitability of each business unit and align sales incentives with internal financial goals.

## Solution

We designed an operational database and a system of dashboards, reports and metrics for daily management. The indicators were built to monitor sales, margins, cash, suppliers, business-unit performance and commercial incentives, with a focus on short-term decisions and financial control.

## Result

The company improved its financial position with suppliers and aligned sales incentives with cash needs and internal profitability goals. Management and middle office teams were also able to focus on more strategic tasks, such as reviewing each business unit and analyzing organic and external investment opportunities for expansion.
