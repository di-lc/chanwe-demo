## Context

The company is an apparel retail brand selling directly to consumers, with strong dependence on promotional campaigns and a cost structure sensitive to customer acquisition costs, payment fees and shipping logistics.

## Problem

After a critical liquidity episode caused by overstock, the company did not have enough funds for advertising, operating payments and broader commitments. Although sales grew in certain periods, customer acquisition costs, shipping costs and payment-management commissions compressed margins until cash flows became tight and weakly profitable.

## Solution

We implemented margin visibility by SKU, category, channel and promotional campaign. Pricing, stock planning and product-promotion criteria were redefined by incorporating full acquisition, payment and logistics costs to estimate the real profitability of each commercial decision.

## Result

The brand achieved positive and stable cash flow, reducing its dependence on seasonal cycles and aggressive promotions. Margins doubled through better SKU promotion choices, pricing adjustments and stock planning aligned with real profitability and cash needs.
