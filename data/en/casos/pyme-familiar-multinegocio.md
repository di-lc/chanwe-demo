## Context

The company is a family-owned SME with three different business units, historically operated through decentralized processes, scattered information and heavy management involvement in low-scale daily tasks.

## Problem

The lack of integrated information prevented the company from properly assessing the economic and financial performance of each business unit. Management was absorbed by daily operational tasks, limiting its ability to negotiate with suppliers, rethink strategy, organize internal responsibilities and decide how to invest excess cash.

## Solution

We centralized information in a single quasi-accounting system, structuring economic and financial performance reports by business unit. With that visibility, operating flows were redesigned, responsibilities were reassigned, employees were trained in the new processes and incentives were aligned with business goals.

## Result

The company transformed its operation through small organizational changes with high impact: clearer responsibilities, team upskilling and less dependence on management in daily operations. This made it possible to focus on supplier negotiation, corporate strategy and investing excess cash in financial and physical assets, diversifying income from operating assets toward more passive assets.
